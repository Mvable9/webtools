GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about resizing your images.

Will resizing reduce my image quality?

Changing image dimensions can affect sharpness, especially when enlarging a small image. The quality setting also affects JPG and WebP output. PNG does not use this quality setting in the same way.

What does maintaining the aspect ratio mean?

It keeps the original relationship between the image width and height, helping prevent your image from looking stretched.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You can also choose one of these formats for your resized output.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This tool processes your image in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to our server.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
