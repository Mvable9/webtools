GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about image compression.

What does an image compressor do?

An image compressor reduces the amount of storage space an image uses. Depending on the format and settings, it may reduce quality or use a more efficient image encoding.

Will compressing my image reduce its quality?

It can. Lower quality settings may produce smaller files but introduce more visible image differences. Higher settings generally preserve more visual detail. Always preview the result before downloading it.

Which image formats are supported?

The tool accepts JPEG, PNG, and WebP images. The browser's image-processing support determines which output formats can be created successfully.

Can I compress a PNG image?

Yes. PNG images can be processed, but choosing PNG output does not guarantee a smaller file. If you need transparency, choose a format that preserves it, such as PNG or supported transparent WebP output.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This tool is designed to process images directly inside your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server for compression.

Why is my compressed image sometimes larger?

Some images are already highly optimized. Changing their format or encoding settings can sometimes increase the resulting file size instead of reducing it.
